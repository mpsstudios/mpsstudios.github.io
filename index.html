About us

A small studio with a big love for play

M.P.S. Studios is an independent mobile game studio based in Athens, Greece. We design, build and publish our own games: bright puzzle and casual experiences that fit into a coffee break and keep you smiling long after.

We care about the details: smooth controls, fair difficulty, cheerful art and music, and a community we listen to.
