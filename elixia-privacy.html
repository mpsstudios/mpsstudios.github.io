Privacy Policy — Elixia: Potion Sort

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Ελληνικά ↓

This policy explains how M.P.S. STUDIOS P.C. (Greece) ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Elixia: Potion Sort (the "Game"). We are the data controller for the Game. You can contact us at any time at mps.studios.pc@gmail.com.

1. Information the Game itself stores

Your game progress (levels, coins, items, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers for the Game, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, for example optional reward videos and ads between levels. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and app-set ID,
  • IP address (used to estimate an approximate location such as country),
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic/crash data related to ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it (for example, which levels are too hard), the Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects app-usage events (levels started and completed, booster and coin use, ads watched, purchases made), an app-instance identifier, device and app information, approximate location (country/region derived from IP) and crash/diagnostic information. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics storage is only enabled if you agree to "store and/or access information on a device" in the consent message; otherwise no analytics identifiers are stored. Data is processed by Google as our service provider and is not used by us to identify you personally.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card or payment details. The Game receives only a purchase confirmation (product and purchase token) so it can deliver your items. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Sharing

When you tap "Share", Android's share menu opens and you choose where to send the text. We do not see what you share. We do not sell personal information.

6. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Providing the Game and delivering purchases: performance of a contract.
  • Personalised advertising and analytics storage in the EEA/UK/CH: your consent (you can withdraw it at any time).
  • Non-personalised ads, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interests.

7. Retention

Progress stays on your device until you uninstall the Game, clear its data, or use Settings → Reset progress. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

8. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests concerning ads can be handled directly through Google and your device settings; for anything else, contact us at mps.studios.pc@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (www.dpa.gr) or your local authority.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
